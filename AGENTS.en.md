## About the project

This Lamborghini project is a study inspired by the digital ecosystem of the luxury automotive manufacturer Lamborghini.

The repository employs a monorepo architecture to integrate a web application and a mobile app, sharing resources, business logic, and integrations wherever appropriate.

The platform aims to replicate and expand upon the digital experience offered by a luxury automotive manufacturer, encompassing features such as a digital showroom, vehicle customization, ordering, customer management, a digital garage, vehicle monitoring and tracking, maintenance, dealership information, and events.

This project is for educational purposes only and does not represent an official application, commercial product, or service authorized by Lamborghini.
